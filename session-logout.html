Session Logout

A lightweight Chrome extension that logs you out of the websites you choose — ChatGPT, Claude, Gmail and more — with a single click. It clears each site's cookies and storage locally. No servers, no tracking, no accounts.

↓ Download Extension (.zip) How to install

Why it exists

Websites stay logged in through their own session cookies — independent of which Google account is signed into your browser. On a shared or work laptop, that means someone can open ChatGPT or Gmail and walk straight into your active session. Session Logout gives you a one-click way to genuinely sign out of the sites you care about, on your terms.

What it does

🧹

One-click logout

Clear cookies, localStorage, IndexedDB, cache and service workers for every site on your list at once.

🎯

Per-site control

Add or remove any domain. Log out of just one site, or all of them. Sites you didn't list are never touched.

🔌

100% local

No backend, no network calls, no telemetry. It only ever deletes session data for sites you choose.

🛡️

Minimal permissions

Built on Manifest V3 with only browsingData and storage permissions — nothing more.

Install it (2 minutes)

  1. Download & unzipGrab the .zip above and extract it to a folder you'll keep (e.g. Documents\SessionLogout).
  2. Open the extensions pageIn Chrome, go to chrome://extensions.
  3. Enable Developer modeToggle it on, top-right. (If it's greyed out, your workplace has blocked unpacked extensions.)
  4. Load unpackedClick Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin & useClick the 🧩 puzzle icon, pin Session Logout, then add your sites and you're set.
Note: Keep the folder where it is — deleting or moving it uninstalls the extension. Unpacked extensions don't auto-update, which is fine for a personal tool.

What it does & doesn't do

  • Clears the local website session for the domains you list
  • Makes those sites ask you to sign in again on next load
  • Runs entirely on your machine with no data leaving the browser
  • It does not read passwords, page content, or your messages
  • It does not remove your Google account from Chrome itself (a browser-level setting)
  • It is not a replacement for locking your screen or full-disk encryption
