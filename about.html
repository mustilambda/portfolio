The short version

I started as a full-time content writer in 2022. Writing hundreds of pages taught me how people search, how intent changes from one query to the next, and why content that ranks but does not move a reader forward is only doing half the job.

That work expanded into SERP analysis, topical architecture, conversion pathways, editorial systems, client strategy and team leadership. Today, I work as an Organic Growth Strategist—building connected SEO, AEO and GEO systems across traditional and AI-driven search.

My focus now: capture qualified search demand, own the questions buyers ask, earn visibility in generative search and connect that attention to measurable business growth.

Writing was the foundation—not the finish line

I've written 300+ pieces across SaaS, travel, lifestyle, packaging, e-commerce and technical niches. That background still matters: I can move from strategy to the page, see where a brief will fail before production begins, and build systems writers can actually follow.

But the work is no longer defined by content volume. I now start with the growth problem: which demand is worth capturing, which questions influence evaluation, which entities and evidence establish authority, and where visibility should lead next.

Why SEO, AEO and GEO belong together

SEO helps a brand get discovered. AEO makes its expertise easy to extract as a direct answer. GEO strengthens the authority, evidence and entity relationships that AI-driven search systems can understand and potentially cite.

I treat them as one organic growth system—not three disconnected services. The goal is consistent: make the brand findable, make the answer useful, and make the path to action clear.

The bit people don't expect

My degree is in Pharmacy. Five years of scientific training made research, source quality and factual precision second nature. It is especially useful when growth work moves into technical, regulated or specialized markets where generic content is not enough.

How I work

I audit the current search footprint, map demand and buyer journeys, prioritize the opportunities closest to business value, and build the SEO + AEO + GEO system around them. Then I measure rankings, acquisition, leads and AI visibility—and iterate.

Writing, editing, content operations and AI-assisted workflows remain execution capabilities. Strategy decides where those capabilities should be used.
