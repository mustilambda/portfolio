The short version

If you Google Mustansar Mahmood, I'm the top result. That's not a coincidence — it's what happens when someone actually understands SEO from the inside out.

I started writing in 2022, covering everything from cryptocurrency to camera gear for international clients. Over time, I moved into leading teams, managing clients, and building content systems that scale. These days I sit somewhere between writer, editor, team lead, and strategist — depending on what the project needs.

I've written 300+ pieces across SaaS, travel, lifestyle, packaging, e-commerce, and academic niches. I've led a 15-writer team, helped brands grow their organic traffic by 2x, and generated over $40,000 in email sales for a single client using Klaviyo campaigns.

The bit people don't expect

My degree is in Pharmacy. Five years of it. That means I'm wired for research, precision, and not publishing something unless the facts are actually correct. That habit carries into everything I write — and everything I edit.

It also means I'm comfortable reading dense material and turning it into something a normal person actually wants to read. Which, it turns out, is exactly what good content writing is.

How I work

I don't disappear after sending a draft. I work closely with whoever I'm working for — whether that's a single client, a content manager, or a full editorial team. I build SOPs so work stays consistent. I flag problems early. I reply quickly.

I also use AI tools (Claude, Grok, Gemini) as part of my writing process — not to replace thinking, but to move faster without cutting corners. Every final piece still goes through my own editorial eye before it goes anywhere.
