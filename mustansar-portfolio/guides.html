How to Write Articles That Rank Higher in Google and Get Featured in AI Overviews

A 2026 framework built around E-E-A-T, semantic relevance, and user intent — so your content earns both traditional rankings and AI citations.

⏱ 8 Steps 📅 2026 Guide 🎯 Informational & Editorial Content
01
Align the Outline with User Intent
Research the exact query and related questions using Google's "People Also Ask," Semrush, or AnswerThePublic. Structure with a clear H1, logical H2/H3s, and sections that directly address what users want. Cover the topic comprehensively so AI doesn't need to pull from multiple sources.
02
Deliver the Direct Answer Up Front
Place the concise, straightforward answer in the first 1–3 sentences or a highlighted summary box. Avoid fluff or long introductions — AI Overviews and users both reward immediate value. Follow with a brief explanation, then go deeper.
03
Use Visuals That Communicate in 3 Seconds
Include concise, engaging infographics, charts, or diagrams that users can understand instantly. Optimise images with descriptive alt text and compress for speed. Multi-modal content (text + visuals) significantly boosts chances of being selected for AI summaries.
04
Add Unique Angles, Fresh Data & Credible Sources
Go beyond generic information. Provide original insights, real-world examples, or first-hand experience. Cite the latest data from government sites, .edu domains, and peer-reviewed studies. Incorporate UGC from Reddit or forums for freshness and relevance.
05
Conduct Deep, Thorough Research
Back every claim with evidence. Reference primary sources, statistics, and expert opinions. Show Experience (real-world cases) and Authority (author bio with credentials). These E-E-A-T signals are what AI systems prioritise when selecting content to surface.
06
Write Simply, Concisely & Clearly
Use short sentences, everyday language, and active voice throughout. Eliminate jargon or explain it immediately. Aim for content that feels conversational yet professional — ideal for both human readers and AI parsing engines.
07
Make Content Highly Scannable
Break up text with bullet points, numbered lists, and comparison tables. Bold key phrases. Keep paragraphs to 2–3 lines max. Use clear transition sentences between sections. Scannable structure signals quality to both users and crawlers.
08
Implement Strong Internal Linking
Link naturally to other relevant articles on your site. This helps users explore more, builds topical authority, and helps Google understand your site's structure — improving both traditional rankings and AI visibility simultaneously.

✦ Bonus Tips for 2026 Success

  • Prioritise long-tail, question-based queries — these frequently trigger AI Overviews and are easier to rank for early.
  • Demonstrate real Experience — include personal insights, case studies, or expert quotes. Generic content gets ignored by AI.
  • Test your content: Does it answer the question completely, clearly, and credibly in under 30 seconds? If not, rewrite.

How to Write Any Commercial Page

Writing great pages is no longer about creativity — it's about research, psychology, and structure. Spend 60–80% of your time researching before you write a single word.

⏱ 5 Phases ✅ Includes Checklist 🎯 Commercial & Landing Pages
▶
Watch first: Video walkthrough of the full commercial page framework
A

Research Phase (60–80% of your total time)

  • Open the page and understand the service/product/brand first (use Grok or similar). Map brand USPs, product USPs, target audience, features, and page goals before anything else.
  • Audience understanding: Think about what users want on this page. Consider their pain points, funnel type, and emotional triggers. Categorise into: Loyal customers (know why you're best), New customers (find their tipping point), and Almost-buyers (currently on the fence). Your copy must speak to all three.
[Advanced] VOC Mining — The Secret Weapon
  • Mine Reddit, Amazon reviews, and forums for exact customer language
  • Interview customers if possible: What triggered the search? What almost stopped them buying? What's life like before vs. after?
  • Organise findings into: pain points, goals, objections, outcomes
  • Competitor analysis: Search the keyword on SERP. Review the headings of the top 10 competitors. Note industry-specific terms and entities they use. Use keyword clustering (group related terms) instead of repeating one keyword.
  • Find direct quotes and UGC videos/reviews — treat them as sticky notes for later.
  • Outline: Make a rough outline, take help from AI to finalise it. Extract headings manually, merge them, and map the user journey (placement of headings). You can use AI to generate a competitor-based outline as a cross-reference.
Rule of Thumb: The more manual the work, the better the result.
B

Writing Phase (30% of your total time)

  • If you can't write the crux of the page in the headline, don't start writing yet.
  • While writing, consider: sales/marketing tone, educational/promotional elements, CTA position, emotional points, industry terms, word count, and page design.
  • No jargon. No tough wording. If a line can be removed without impacting quality, remove it.
  • Less content, more visualisation.

Writing Techniques That Convert


  • Strong and descriptive headlines — 80% of people read only this
  • Benefits over features throughout the entire content
  • Active voice only — no "whether/while" sentence constructions
  • Eliminate AI clichés: "transform," "leverage," "imagine," "picture this," em dashes
  • Write in 1–3 sentence standalone blocks; 120–180 words per section maximum
  • Each section must make sense on its own — no context dependency
  • Use bullets and tables — AI loves structured data
  • Definitely add reviews as trust factors
C

CTA Rules

  • No generic CTAs — every CTA must be specific and action-oriented
  • Use multiple CTAs with the same intent (different intent = separate pages)
  • Use different word choice across CTAs even when intent is the same
  • Include phone numbers, WhatsApp links, or direct contact options where possible
  • Use sales, promotions, or limited-time offers to add urgency
  • One CTA immediately after the hero and one after the last section — non-negotiable
D

Location Page Rules

  • H1 must include the location name
  • Embed Google Map (optional but recommended for local trust)
  • Mention local landmarks to reinforce geo-relevance
  • Use NAP — Name, Address, Phone — consistently throughout the page
  • Include location-specific services, not just generic ones
E

FAQ Rules

  • Get FAQs from SERP's "People Also Ask" — the single most credible source
  • FAQ type depends on intent: use MOFU + BOFU FAQs for collection pages; BOFU FAQs only for product and home pages
  • Keep answers under 30 words — no padding
  • Write in a direct tone; always be certain, never ambiguous
  • Bullets are fine inside FAQ answers if needed for clarity
