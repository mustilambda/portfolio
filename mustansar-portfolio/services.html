Services

What I can do
for your brand.

I work with businesses, agencies, and individuals across content, strategy, and team management. Here's everything I offer.

01

SEO Content Writing

Long-form blogs, pillar pages, and landing pages written to rank on Google and actually get read. I've written 300+ pieces across SaaS, travel, lifestyle, packaging, e-commerce, and academic niches — with proper keyword placement, semantic structure, and no fluff padding. Every piece is researched, structured with clear headings, and edited before delivery.

Blog posts Landing pages Pillar content How-to guides
02

Content Strategy

Keyword research, topical authority mapping, competitor analysis, and content calendars — built around what your brand actually needs to achieve. I don't just hand you a keyword list. I tell you what to write, in what order, and why — so your content builds authority over time instead of sitting in isolation.

Keyword clustering Topical maps Content calendars Competitor gaps
03

Editing & Proofreading

I've proofread and edited work from teams of up to 15 writers simultaneously. I know exactly what bad content looks like and how to fix it fast — without rewriting everything from scratch. I check for clarity, flow, keyword usage, factual accuracy, and brand voice consistency. Clean, consistent, on-brand.

Copy editing Structural edits SEO checks Proofreading
04

Team Leadership & Management

I've built, trained, and managed content teams from scratch. I create SOPs that actually get followed, run QA systems that keep output consistent, and manage editorial workflows without micromanaging everyone to death. If you need someone to take ownership of a content team — not just contribute to it — this is the service for you.

Team building Writer training SOP creation QA systems
05

Copywriting

Emails, social posts, landing pages, ad copy, and VSLs. Copy that converts is a different skill from copy that just reads well — I aim to do both. I've run Klaviyo email campaigns that generated $40,000+ in sales for a single client. If you need copy that drives action, not just engagement, let's talk.

Email sequences Ad copy Social posts VSLs
06

AI-Assisted Writing

I use Claude, Grok, and Gemini as part of my workflow — not as a crutch, but as a tool. The thinking, strategy, and editorial judgment are still human. What AI does is make the process faster and the output more consistent. If you want the efficiency of AI without sacrificing quality, this is exactly the sweet spot I work in.

AI-human hybrid Quality control Faster delivery Consistent output
